## 4. Root Cause of Previous 95% Recall at 13 Words, 1 Substitution:
- Arithmetic of intact 13-word window: 13 words produce 13 - 5 + 1 = 9 total 5-grams.
- A single substitution at index 6 destroys exactly 5 sliding 5-grams (grams [2, 3, 4, 5, 6]).
- Intra-page arithmetic: 9 - 5 = 4 distinct 5-grams survive (grams [0, 1, 7, 8]), guaranteeing 100% detection under the >= 3 threshold.
- Isolated failing instance from global word list: when a 13-word passage straddles across a Wikisource page boundary, inter-page 5-grams do not exist in the target bank. In that case, only 7 valid target 5-grams exist initially; subtracting 5 destroyed grams leaves only 2 surviving grams, resulting in a false miss.
